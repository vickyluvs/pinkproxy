Founder story

Hi, I'm Victoria.

I came up through hands-on cybersecurity training: digital forensics, network analysis, cloud infrastructure and offensive security labs. Along the way I noticed something. The women-owned businesses around me were building real companies with real client data, and almost none of them had anyone helping with security.

They weren't careless. They were busy, and every option on the table was either a pricey IT retainer or advice full of jargon. So the basics slipped: shared passwords, ex-contractors who still had access, domains anyone could impersonate.

I started The Pink Proxy to be the security partner I wish every founder had: calm, clear, and on your side.

My focus is identity and access, the area where small teams carry the most risk and get the fastest wins. Every engagement is grounded in the same public frameworks that protect much larger organizations, sized to fit a team of three to ten.

Who we work best with

For founders who take their clients' trust seriously.

We work best with women business owners who'd rather prevent a problem than recover from one, and who want to understand their systems, not just outsource them. If you value clear explanations, steady progress and a business built to last, we'll get along.

We're a great fit if you

  • Treat your clients' money and data as a responsibility
  • Would rather spend an hour now than a week recovering later
  • Want to understand what changed, not just hear "it's fixed"
  • Want to run your own systems with confidence afterward
  • See security as part of building a business that lasts

We're probably not the right fit if you want

  • The cheapest option, whatever the result
  • Someone to take over completely, with no involvement from you
  • Penetration testing, hacking or a compliance certificate. We'll happily point you to someone who does that.
