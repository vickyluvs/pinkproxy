About The Pink Proxy

Founder story

Hi, I'm Victoria Smith, founder of The Pink Proxy.

My background includes hands-on cybersecurity training in digital forensics, network analysis, cloud infrastructure, and security labs. I started The Pink Proxy after seeing women business owners manage client data, growing teams, and everyday technology without clear security support.

I started The Pink Proxy to be the security partner I wish every founder had: calm, clear, and on your side.

My focus is making account access and everyday security easier to understand: who can sign in, what they can access, and what needs to change when someone joins or leaves.

You'll get a clearly scoped project, plain-English explanations, and a walkthrough of the agreed work, so you understand what changed and what to do next.

How we work

What working with The Pink Proxy looks like

  • Pricing

    Fixed-price projects with scope agreed before work begins. Optional ongoing care is separate.

  • Communication

    Plain-English findings, clear priorities, and a walkthrough.

  • Accounts

    Review shared logins and implement agreed individual access and password-management safeguards.

  • Employee changes

    A documented process for granting and removing access, where included in the selected package.

  • Email

    Review authentication settings; configuration work depends on the package.

  • Documentation

    Record completed work and map relevant recommendations to public security frameworks.

  • Your role

    Stay informed and retain ownership of your business systems.
