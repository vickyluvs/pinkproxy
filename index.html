Sound familiar?

The three gaps we see in almost every growing small team.

None of these mean you did something wrong. They happen when a business grows faster than its setup. Here is how we close each one.

The problem

Passwords shared over Slack

The Shopify login lives in a DM from 2023. Three people know the bank password. Nobody is sure who can see what.

The Pink Proxy fix

A shared vault and one login per person

We set up a business password manager, turn on multi-factor sign-in, and give each person access only to the tools their role needs.

The problem

Worry about an abrupt exit

When someone leaves on a bad day, you scramble to remember every account they could still open.

The Pink Proxy fix

A documented offboarding playbook for your apps

A written, step-by-step exit checklist for your exact apps, plus an access map so you can lock everything down in one sitting.

The problem

Someone is spoofing your domain

A client gets an invoice "from you" with new bank details. Your real emails start landing in spam.

The Pink Proxy fix

SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up correctly

We configure email authentication to help receiving systems identify unauthorized use of your domain.

The framework

Five pillars. One solid foundation.

Every engagement works through the same five areas, each tied to the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 so your progress is measurable and described in terms insurers, partners and larger clients may recognize. Framework mapping is not a certification.

Identity & Access

Who can sign in to what, and how.

  • Multi-factor sign-in on every account
  • Business password manager
  • Joiner and leaver runbooks

Domain & Email Defense

Reduce the risk of email impersonation.

  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC records
  • Registrar lock and recovery contacts
  • Phishing filters tuned
CSF 2.0PR.PSDE.CM

Endpoint Hardening

Strengthen security settings on business laptops and phones.

  • Disk encryption and screen lock
  • Automatic updates confirmed
  • Device inventory

Network & Data

Your files, your Wi-Fi, and where client data lives.

  • Router firmware and guest Wi-Fi separation
  • Sharing permissions cleaned up
  • Backups that are actually tested
